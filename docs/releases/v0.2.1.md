Huddle 0.2.1 brings the agent CLIs on your computer into Huddle, and makes the Linux app at home on Arch
and [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org).

## Agent CLIs as assistants
Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode, signed in as you already use them, can now be assistants in your flows
(pick **Agent CLI on this computer**). Huddle asks each one the careful way: in an empty folder of its own,
without your MCP servers, and in that CLI's most limited mode. Claude Code may only search the web, Codex
runs in its read-only sandbox, and OpenCode answers without tools, so none of them can change anything on
your computer.

## On Arch and Omarchy
- **An Arch package** below: `sudo pacman -U ./huddle-bin-0.2.1-1-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst`. It goes on the AUR
  once the AUR takes new accounts again.
- **Huddle wears your Omarchy theme**, and changes with it when you switch.
- **Approvals with a click**: where notifications have no buttons (Omarchy), clicking one opens what's
  waiting in Huddle, even from the notification history.
- **Bind a key to quick ask** where the desktop keeps global shortcuts to itself (Hyprland, other Wayland
  desktops): `huddle --quick`. The README has the Omarchy lines.

## The Linux app
- `huddle --approvals` opens what's waiting, `huddle --restart` restarts the server, and running `huddle`
  again always brings the window back, even when it was minimized on Wayland.
- Where there's no tray (stock GNOME), closing the window minimizes it instead of hiding it.
- The quick-ask box picks up assistants you added while it was closed.

Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`. The app is unsigned for now.
