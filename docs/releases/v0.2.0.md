Huddle 0.2.0 is the first release with a **Linux app**, and a lot more since 0.1.0.

## The Linux app
- Lives in the tray and starts with your session, so schedules, email and other triggers keep running.
- **Ctrl+Alt+H** opens a quick-ask box; the answer comes back as text, a table, a chart or a calculator.
- Approvals arrive as notifications you can answer with Yes or No.
- Your keys live in one file only you can read. Download the `.deb` or the AppImage below.

## Meet the Huddlers
A little bot for each of your assistants (OpenClaw has claws, Hermes wings, Ollama llama ears…) and Hud,
Huddle's own. They show who answered, think while a step runs, and keep you company on empty screens.

## Answers you can interact with
Tables, bar, line and part-to-whole charts, flowcharts, calculators and weighted comparisons. Jev or your
model chooses the view and fills it in **as checked JSON that Huddle draws itself: never code a model wrote**.

## Spending less
Prices per assistant and real token counts; "auto" takes the cheapest assistant that fits nearly as well;
conversations stick to one assistant (warm prompt caches); answers can be given again; budgets.

## More
- **vLLM**: bring your own model, with its model list and `--api-key`.
- **Huddle as an MCP server**: your flows as tools in Claude Code, Claude Desktop and Cursor.
- **Guards, optional**: check every hand-off with your own policy service (`HUDDLE_GUARD=http`) or
  ArmorIQ (`HUDDLE_GUARD=armoriq`).
- **Trust tiers**: contacts get their own tokens; strangers are turned away or wait for your OK.
- **PACT sign-in** for businesses, **email** in and out through Resend, **memory** search.
- Three new starter flows: inbox digest, morning brief, second opinion. Every example is tested end to end.

Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`. The app is unsigned for now.
